Bundled dictionaries — vendored, do not edit by hand.
=====================================================

These three Yomitan zips ship next to the binary (build.rs copies this
directory to target/{profile}/assets/dictionaries/, and build_appimage.sh
bundles assets/* into the AppImage). On first start the app imports each one
into dictionary.sqlite, once, and marks it built-in; see
src/data/bundled.rs. Nothing is downloaded at runtime.

Every pin below was verified with sha256sum against the downloaded artifact.
