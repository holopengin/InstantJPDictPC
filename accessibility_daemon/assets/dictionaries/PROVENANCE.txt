Bundled dictionary — vendored, do not edit by hand.
===================================================

The Kanjium pitch accents zip ships next to the binary (build.rs copies this
directory to target/{profile}/assets/dictionaries/, and build_appimage.sh
bundles assets/* into the AppImage). On first start the app imports it into
dictionary.sqlite, once, and marks it built-in; see src/data/bundled.rs. The
row is not deletable from the settings manager. Nothing is downloaded.

Jitendex and KANJIDIC are NOT bundled: they are one-click downloads from the
pinned catalog in assets/catalog/dictionaries.json (mirroring mobile's
assets/catalog/dictionaries.json).
